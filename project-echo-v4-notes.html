◎ Project Echo

Speaker Notes — Hack Week 2026
Estimated delivery: 5–6 minutes. Speak at a comfortable, unhurried pace. Pauses are your friend — they signal confidence, not hesitation.
Slide 01
Title — Project Echo ~25 sec

Walk to the front. Let the slide sit for two full seconds before you say anything.

Every business is built on promises. Promises made in contracts, in emails, in phone calls. Promises about what a customer will pay, what discount they agreed to, what happens when it is time to renew. These promises are the commercial foundation of every relationship we have. And right now, we are losing them — not in one big failure, but quietly. One missed email. One discount that silently runs past its expiry. One conversation that only one person ever knew about, and they left six months ago. Project Echo is here to fix that. For good.

Move forward without adding anything else.

Slide 02
The Challenge — Five Systems ~40 sec

Point to each system as you name it.

A renewal question arrives — something simple. Can this customer keep their discount? And here is what actually happens today. We open HubSpot, and find a note that says: see email. We open the email thread — it is 48 messages deep with no resolution. We find the PDF contract, buried in section seven. We find a Slack message from someone who already left — just to clarify the discount is temp, by the way — which is, of course, legally meaningless. And finally, we find a spreadsheet called v3-FINAL-v2-UPDATED-USE-THIS.

Five systems. Manual reconstruction. Every single time. And here is the thing — this is not a process failure. It is a structural one. Commercial intent was never designed to be captured. It was assumed someone would remember. That assumption is creating friction — and we can do better.

Slide 03
The Moment of Truth ~40 sec

Make eye contact with the room. Ask the question directly — do not rush it.

Right now — today — for any of our active clients, can anyone tell me with confidence whether their current discount was agreed to be permanent, or temporary?

Four full seconds of silence. Let it be a little uncomfortable.

If the person who ran that deal is in this room, maybe yes. If they are on holiday — probably not. If they left six months ago — definitely not. This is the question behind every renewal conversation we have. And right now, the honest answer is: we do not know quickly enough, safely enough. That is exactly the opportunity Echo addresses.

Slide 04
The Opportunity ~45 sec

The good news is that this is entirely fixable. Every challenge on this slide is an opportunity — to protect revenue, to strengthen our commercial position, to make our team more resilient.

Pricing agreements that drift from their original intent are an opportunity to ensure every deal we make is honoured exactly as agreed. Verbal commitments that were never formalised are an opportunity to make our emails and contracts watertight from the start. And the reliance on individual people — that is the opportunity to move from a model where knowledge lives in a person, to one where knowledge lives in the system.

Gesture toward the before/after comparison on the right.

Think about the person on your team who holds the most commercial context. Now imagine they are away for two weeks, or they have just handed in their notice. Project Echo means that scenario has no impact on our ability to renew confidently.

Slide 05
Two Modes ~30 sec

So — this is Project Echo. It works in two modes. Sales Mode is about winning deals correctly from the very start — capturing commercial terms properly, checking contract language before anything goes out, and drafting confirmation emails that are clear, professional, and legally sound. Renewal Mode is about going into every renewal conversation with complete intelligence — no archaeology, no guesswork, full context. Let me show you both in action.

Slide 06
Echo Sales — Deal Capture (ELTE Budapest) ~50 sec

Point to the left column first, then move across.

Here is a new deal being captured in Echo — ELTE Budapest, our education sector partnership. The sales rep enters the deal details: the rate, the discount, the rationale. And before anything goes out the door, Echo reviews the contract.

Notice what it finds. Clause 4.2 uses the phrase payment upfront — for a subscription model, that should say annual payment in advance. A small thing, but it matters for billing consistency. Clause 7.1 is missing the discount duration entirely. The single most common source of renewal disputes is a discount with no documented end date. Echo catches this before the agreement is ever sent, and tells us exactly what the clause should say.

Nothing leaves our hands until it is right. That is the principle.

Slide 07
Echo Drafts — Email & Contract Clause ~45 sec

From the same commercial data — the same deal terms — Echo produces two completely different documents. On the left, the confirmation email: clear, warm, professional, written for a person. On the right, the contract clause: formal, precise, written for a document that will be relied upon legally.

Both capture exactly the same commercial intent — the pricing, the discount duration, the uplift mechanism. The email says it in plain English. The contract says it in enforceable terms. And both are ready in seconds. No drafting from scratch. No risk of the email saying one thing and the contract saying another. And if the customer has different payment terms from our standard agreement, Echo can generate and download that amendment directly from here too.

Slide 08
Clean State — When Echo Was There from Day One ~30 sec

This is the destination. ELTE Budapest was onboarded with Echo in place. The discount duration is in clause 2.3. The uplift mechanism is in clause 2.4. The auto-renewal term is confirmed. So when the CS manager opens Echo at renewal twelve months later, the response is: zero review points. Renewal email ready to send. No archaeology. No uncertainty. No risk.

This is what renewal looks like when the groundwork was done right. Now let me show you what Echo can help us improve in situations where that groundwork is less complete.

Slide 09
Commercial Summary — University of Passau ~60 sec

Point to each column in turn.

Here is the University of Passau — Germany — an account where not all of the commercial context was captured at the point of sale. Echo cross-references the contract, the sales emails, and the CRM notes, and presents a complete picture in three equal parts.

The commercial summary tells us the facts: 20% discount applied, 18,400 covered lives, annual value of £38,640. There is a real opportunity here — at standard rate, that is an additional £12,400 of ARR we can move toward.

The quality review column shows us where we can strengthen the commercial record — the discount end date was not formally documented, and the uplift mechanism is absent. These are straightforward improvements Echo helps us make right now.

And the third column is particularly important. This contract has a mutual agreement renewal clause — which means renewal is not automatic. Echo interprets the clause, reads the tone of recent emails, and advises the CSM on the right level of documentation. This customer is cooperative, so a formal letter works. But for the pricing change, a signed variation gives us the strongest protection. Echo makes that recommendation clearly.

Slide 10
Pricing Calculator ~50 sec

Interact with the calculator live — change the lives count, then the discount.

Before we decide what actions are needed, we need to decide what we actually want to offer. This is a live calculator. Let us say the University of Passau has grown — I will update the lives to 20,000. The invoice updates instantly. Now let us say we want to offer a 10% loyalty discount — I enter it here. Everything recalculates: annual value, monthly invoice, exact line items, effective rate per life.

Echo also tells me whether my discount is commercially sound — green at standard rate, amber if we are in the moderate range, and if I go above 20 per cent, Echo immediately prompts me to generate the variation.

If showing the modal: click the button. Point to the two upload zones.

I upload the client's existing contract and our company template — Echo completes the required fields and the variation is ready to download for signature. Directly from here. No switching systems.

Slide 11
Variation & Governance ~35 sec

Echo does not just calculate — it governs the paperwork. And the scope is broader than pricing alone. A customer asking for a five-year term, a change to payment structure, an extension of a fixed-term contract — all of these may need a formal variation. Echo identifies the trigger, tells us exactly what the variation must include, generates the document from our template, and makes it available to download for signature. The paper trail is complete from day one through every renewal.

Slide 12
The Echo Advantage ~50 sec

Let me put this in practical terms. Today, answering a single renewal query takes up to 45 minutes of manual research across five systems. With Echo, that is under a minute. At scale — Echo can review and summarise 100 contracts in under two hours. An audit that used to take days becomes an afternoon task.

But the impact I am most excited about is what this means for our team. New CS and Sales team members can be fully operational from day one. No months of shadowing. No digging through old folders and email chains. Open Echo, and the complete commercial history of every account is right there — accurate, structured, ready to use. That means new team members can focus from their very first week on what actually moves the needle: engaging clients, exploring new markets, reviewing growth opportunities.

And with Echo in place across the organisation, commercial and legal errors approach zero — not because people are more careful, but because the system makes the right thing the easy thing.

Slide 13
What's Next — Future Vision ~25 sec

The platform vision is close in and practical. Voice recording means verbal commitments made on calls are automatically converted into structured commercial records. HubSpot auto-update means the CRM always reflects the latest picture without anyone manually entering data. Smart document search means finding any clause, any agreement, any pricing history takes seconds. And Echo generates the finance reports our finance team needs — renewal pipeline, billing forecasts, revenue by contract — on demand. This is a full commercial intelligence layer for the organisation.

Closing ~20 sec

Slow right down. Take a breath before you begin.

Right now, our commercial memory lives in scattered notes and individual employees. Project Echo turns that fragmented knowledge into a searchable, intelligent system — one that captures commercial intent at the point of sale, governs it through every renewal, and ensures every promise — every discount, every clause, every agreement — is remembered. Permanently.

Every commercial promise remembered.
