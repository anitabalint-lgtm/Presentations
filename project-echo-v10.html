Contract Clause — Clause 2
2.2 For the Initial Subscription Term commencing 1 September 2026, a 25% education sector discount is applied to the standard rate of £2.625 per covered life, giving an effective rate of £1.969 per life for 8,500 covered lives. Annual contract value: £16,737, payable annually in advance.

2.3 This discount applies to the Initial Subscription Term only and expires at the end of that term. Standard pricing of £2.625 per life applies from Year 2 unless separately agreed in writing prior to renewal.

2.4 Annual uplift of RPI+2% applies from Year 2, with 60 days' written notice.
