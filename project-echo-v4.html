Echo · Variation & Governance

Terms changed? Echo generates the paperwork.

Pricing, subscription term, contract duration, payment structure, or termination conditions — any change may need a formal variation. Echo handles it.

What triggers a variation
  • 📝Price change from the original contract terms
  • 📝Renewal of a fixed-term contract (not auto-renew)
  • 📝Customer requesting a 5-year term with termination clause + refund schedule
  • 📝Payment structure change (e.g. quarterly instead of annual)
  • 📝Addition of new services or a change to covered-life thresholds
What the variation must include
  • ✅ Updated price per life and effective date
  • ✅ Confirmation of any discount expiry
  • ✅ Revised annual contract value
  • ✅ Confirmation all other terms remain unchanged
  • ✅ Signature block for both parties
