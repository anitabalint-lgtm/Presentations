◎ Project Echo

Speaker Notes — Hack Week 2026
Target delivery: ~3 minutes. Keep it conversational. The slides carry the detail — your job is to connect the dots simply and confidently.
Slide 01
Title — Echo ~20 sec

Walk to the front. Let the slide breathe for a moment before speaking.

This is Project Echo. It is a commercial intelligence tool that helps our CS and Sales teams centralise information, search it instantly, get smart suggestions, and create documentation — all in one place. The goal is simple: confidence, clarity, and commercially sound decisions, every time.

Slide 02
The Opportunity ~30 sec

Right now, when a renewal question comes in, the answer might be spread across five different systems — a CRM note, an email thread, a PDF contract, a Slack message, and a spreadsheet. We can do this better and faster. Echo brings all of that together into one place. The result: the team works faster, more accurately, and with much more confidence going into every client conversation.

Slide 03
What Echo Does ~25 sec

Echo does four things. It centralises all commercial information — contracts, emails, CRM data, pricing — in one searchable system. It searches that information instantly, so no one is digging through folders. It suggests the right next step, whether that is a contract clause, a renewal action, or a pricing decision. And it documents everything — drafting emails, contract clauses, and variation agreements automatically.

Slide 04
Echo Sales Mode — ELTE Budapest ~35 sec

Here is Echo in Sales Mode, using a real example — ELTE Budapest, our education sector partnership. The sales rep enters the deal details, and before anything goes out the door, Echo reviews the contract. It points to specific clauses — for example, clause 7.1 is missing a discount end date, and clause 4.2 uses the wrong billing term. Echo tells us exactly what the language should say.

Then, from the same deal data, Echo drafts both the confirmation email and the contract clause — professional, clear, and commercially sound. Nothing leaves our hands until it is right.

Slide 05
Echo Renewal Mode — University of Passau ~40 sec

Walk through the calculator live — update the lives and discount fields as you speak.

Now in Renewal Mode. The University of Passau — Germany. Echo cross-references the contract, emails, and CRM and gives us a complete commercial summary instantly. I can update the number of lives and the discount rate here, and the invoice recalculates in real time. Echo also tells me whether my discount needs a formal variation — and if it does, I can upload the client contract and our template right here, and Echo generates the variation document ready to download and send for signature.

Echo also reads the renewal clause and the tone of recent emails, and advises the CSM on the safest way to confirm the renewal — whether that is an email, a letter, or a signed amendment. No guesswork. Just a clear recommendation.

Slide 06
The Echo Difference ~25 sec

The numbers speak for themselves. A renewal query that used to take 45 minutes now takes under a minute. Echo can review 100 contracts in under two hours. And new team members — in CS or Sales — are fully operational from day one. No months of shadowing. No digging through old files. They can focus immediately on what matters: engaging clients, exploring new markets, and growing the business.

Slide 07
What's Next ~20 sec

And the platform grows from here. Voice recording turns verbal commitments into structured records automatically. HubSpot sync keeps the CRM always up to date. Smart search means finding any clause or pricing history takes seconds. And Echo generates the finance reports — renewal pipeline, billing forecasts, revenue by contract — on demand. A full commercial intelligence layer for the whole organisation.

Slide 08
Closing ~15 sec

Slow down. Pause before the final line.

Project Echo turns fragmented knowledge into a system the whole team can rely on. It supports our people, protects our commercial agreements, and brings ambiguity closer to zero. From the moment a deal is won, through every renewal, every promise is remembered.

Pause. Then say just this — nothing else.

Every commercial promise remembered.
