Echo · Variation & Governance

Terms changed? Echo generates the paperwork.

Not just pricing — any change to subscription term, contract duration, payment structure, or termination conditions may require a formal variation.

What triggers a variation?
  • 📝Price change beyond original contract terms
  • 📝Renewal of fixed-term contract (not auto-renew)
  • 📝Customer requesting 5-year term with termination clause + refund schedule
  • 📝Payment structure change (e.g. quarterly instead of annual)
  • 📝Addition of new services or covered-life threshold
What the variation must include
  • ✅ Updated price per life and effective date
  • ✅ Expiry statement for any temporary discount
  • ✅ Revised annual contract value
  • ✅ Confirmation that all other terms remain unchanged
  • ✅ Signature block for both parties
