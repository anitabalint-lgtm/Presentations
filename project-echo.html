Dear Sarah,


I'm writing ahead of your 1 July renewal to outline the revised pricing for the upcoming contract year.


When we agreed your current rate of £2.10 per life, this reflected a temporary hardship discount applied during an exceptionally difficult period for your organisation. As noted in our agreement at the time, this arrangement was intended for Year 1 only.


For the 2025–26 contract year, we are proposing a revised rate of £2.40 per life, representing a modest and commercially reasonable adjustment that remains below standard rate card. This reflects both the continued partnership and our mutual commitment to sustainable pricing.
